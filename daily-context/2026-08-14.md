## Part 2 新题

### P2-09 电子产品故障 · 物品/法律/食物/规则类
Describe a problem you had with a technology product,like a computer or a phone.
- What the technology product was
- What was the problem
- Whether you fixed the problem in the end
- And explain how you felt about this experience.
- 迁移骨架：烟花禁令、CCD相机、生日蛋糕、科技产品、动物故事、广告、电影与节目

### P2-15 社媒有趣视频 · 事件/经历/计划类
Describe an interesting video that you watched on the internet.
- What it was about
- When and where you watched it
- How you found out about this it
- And explain why you thought this video was interesting.
- 迁移骨架：学业压力与心理治愈、长期志向、计划改变、运动休闲、家庭节日与成长

### P2-22 久等回复的时刻 · 事件/经历/计划类
Describe a time when you wrote a message or email to someone,but you waited a long time for the person to reply.
- When this happened
- Who you sent the message to
- Why you wrote to this person
- And explain how you felt about waiting a long time for this person to reply.
- 迁移骨架：学业压力与心理治愈、长期志向、计划改变、运动休闲、家庭节日与成长

### P2-26 禁用手机的场合 · 事件/经历/计划类
Describe a time when you were not allowed to use your phone.
- When it was
- Where you were
- Why you couldn't use it
- And explain how you felt about it
- 迁移骨架：学业压力与心理治愈、长期志向、计划改变、运动休闲、家庭节日与成长

### P2-31 常用的软件 · 物品/法律/食物/规则类
Describe a program or app that you like using on your computer or phone.
- What it is and what it is used for
- How you knew it
- When you use it
- And explain why you like using this program or app.
- 迁移骨架：烟花禁令、CCD相机、生日蛋糕、科技产品、动物故事、广告、电影与节目

### P2-42 想拥有的科技产品 · 物品/法律/食物/规则类
Describe a piece oftechnology (not a phone)that you would like to buy.
- What it is
- How much it is
- What you will use it for
- And explain why you would like to buy this piece of technology.
- 迁移骨架：烟花禁令、CCD相机、生日蛋糕、科技产品、动物故事、广告、电影与节目
