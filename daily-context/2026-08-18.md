## Part 2 新题

### P2-08 当地新闻 · 事件/经历/计划类
Describe a piece oflocal news in your area that people are interested in
- What it was about
- Where you saw/heard it
- Who was involved
- And explain why people were interested in it
- 迁移骨架：学业压力与心理治愈、长期志向、计划改变、运动休闲、家庭节日与成长

### P2-19 想实施的环保法律 · 物品/法律/食物/规则类
Describe a law to help the environment which you think your country should have.
- What it is
- Why your country needs this law
- Whether this law would be popular
- And explain why this law would help the environment.
- 迁移骨架：烟花禁令、CCD相机、生日蛋糕、科技产品、动物故事、广告、电影与节目

### P2-48 鼓励环保的人 · 人物类
Describe a person you know who encourages people to protect the environment or nature.
- Who the person is
- How this person encourages people to protect the environment or nature.
- How long has this person been doing this
- And explain how you feel about this person who encourages people to protect the environment or nature.
- 迁移骨架：闺蜜 Lily、爷爷、妹妹、爸爸、暗恋学长、宠物博主、兽医学姐
